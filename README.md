## Purpose

The Online Resilience Kit was created to make practical resilience knowledge freely available to anyone who may find it useful.

The goal is simple:

- Increase individual and community resilience
- Support preparedness and adaptive thinking
- Encourage practical action over fear
- Share knowledge without financial barriers
- Help people navigate uncertainty with confidence and compassion

This project is offered as a public service and community resource.

---

## Why This Exists

The future is becoming increasingly complex.

Many people recognize the need to become more resilient but do not know where to begin. Information is often scattered, overly technical, locked behind paywalls, or mixed with fear-based messaging.

The Online Resilience Kit was created to provide accessible, actionable resources that support thoughtful preparation, self-reliance, and community connection.
